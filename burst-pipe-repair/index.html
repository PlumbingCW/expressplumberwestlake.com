Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Westlake, OH

A burst pipe can release hundreds of gallons in a short time. Call (440) 468-0237, 24/7. First, shut the main water valve fully off, open a faucet low in the house and stay out of rooms where water is near electrical outlets.

Most burst pipes in Northeast Ohio follow a cold snap. Water in an unheated space freezes and expands, and the pipe splits where the ice builds pressure. The leak often shows only after the pipe thaws, which is why a damp ceiling can appear hours after the cold ends.

We stop the leak, repair or replace the damaged section and check the rest of the run for weak spots. If the damage is widespread, we say so plainly and talk through the options.

What to do in the first minutes

  1. Find the main shutoff, usually where the water line enters near the meter, and turn it fully off.
  2. Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain out what is left in the pipes.
  3. Turn off power only if you can reach the breaker from a dry spot. Leave the house if water is near the panel.
  4. Move belongings out of the wet area and call us.

Cleveland Water's guidance says to label the main shutoff in advance and to call a licensed plumber if a pipe bursts. If a frozen pipe starts leaking as it thaws, shut off the water to that section or to the whole house.

Why pipes burst and where

Unheated spaces

Garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls are the usual places for a freeze.

Outdoor spigots

A hose left on over a hard freeze can split the spigot and the pipe behind it.

Water hammer and age

Old galvanized and aging copper split at weak points even without a freeze.

Pressure spikes

A failed pressure regulator or relief can overload old fittings.

Thawing without making it worse

If a pipe is frozen but not yet leaking, open the faucet it feeds, warm the room and apply gentle heat with a hair dryer, heating pad or space heater placed safely. Cleveland Water's guidance is clear that a blow torch or open flame should never be used, because of the fire risk and damage to the pipe.

If you cannot find the frozen section or cannot reach it, call a plumber. A pipe that has frozen once has been stressed and may burst when it thaws, so be ready at the shutoff.

Repair and prevention

Repair choices run from a clamp or coupling on a split section to replacing a whole run. After repairs, we suggest insulating exposed pipes and sealing cold air leaks. Recurring problems on old lines are discussed on the repiping page, and leaks you have not located yet are the job of leak detection.

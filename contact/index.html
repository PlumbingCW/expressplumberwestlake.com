What to tell dispatch

  • Your street address and ZIP code, so we can confirm coverage.
  • What is happening: where the water or sewage is, and whether it is still running.
  • Whether you have shut off the main valve or the fixture valve.
  • Whether the home is a house, townhouse or a business, and roughly how old it is.
  • A phone number we can reach if the call drops.

While you wait

Shut off water at the main if it is flowing and you cannot stop it. Stop using drains if they are backing up. Keep people away from wet electrical areas. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.

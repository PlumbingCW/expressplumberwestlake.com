A clogged drain in Westlake usually starts small. A sink empties a little slower, a tub holds a ring of gray water, or a basement floor drain gives off a smell after laundry. By the time it becomes a call, the blockage has typically been building for weeks.

Drain cleaning is the fastest way to restore flow. We run a cable machine through the line, break up or retrieve what is blocking it, and then check that the water runs freely. Most single-fixture clogs are cleared in one visit.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

Slow drains can wait for a scheduled visit. Some situations should not wait, and dispatch treats them as urgent calls.

Treat it as an emergency when

  • Water is coming back up. A tub, shower or floor drain is filling when you run a different fixture.
  • Every drain is slow at once. That points to the main line rather than one fixture. See sewer line cleaning.
  • There is a sewage smell. Raw sewage carries bacteria, so keep children and pets out of the room.
  • A toilet has overflowed. Stop the flow at the shutoff behind the toilet and call right away.

What causes clogs in Westlake homes

Kitchen lines clog with grease, coffee grounds and food scraps. Bathroom lines clog with hair and soap film. Laundry lines clog with lint and fabric softener residue. Floor drains in basements collect silt and debris, especially in a house that has seen water on the floor.

Older homes add a second layer of causes. Cast iron drains develop rough interior scale that catches paper and grease, and long horizontal runs in a basement can sag slightly so water pauses and leaves solids behind. Tree roots can enter an older clay or cast iron lateral at a joint. If a camera is needed to see what the cable is hitting, we recommend a sewer camera inspection.

How we clear a drain

We start with questions. Which fixtures are affected, how long it has been happening and what went down the drain recently all narrow the search. Then we locate a cleanout or pull the trap and run a cable machine sized to the pipe.

The cable head cuts through grease and roots or hooks what is blocking the line. We flush the line with water, confirm the drain runs at full speed and tell you what we found. If the line keeps clogging, the honest answer may be that cable work is only a temporary fix. In that case hydro jetting scours the pipe wall, and camera work shows whether the pipe itself is damaged.

Backup or overflow right now: what to do first

Stop using water in the house. Every flush and every sink adds to the problem. Shut the water off at the fixture if it is still running, and move rugs and stored items away from the floor drain.

Why chemical openers make things worse

Chemical drain openers sit in the pipe above a blockage and can eat at old metal pipe, damage some plastics and splash on whoever opens the trap next. If one has already been used, tell the plumber. It changes how the line is handled. A cable machine clears the pipe without that risk.

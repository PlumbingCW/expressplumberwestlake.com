What to do in the first five minutes of a plumbing emergency

Most of the damage in a plumbing emergency happens after the first few minutes, not during them. A few quick actions cost nothing and often decide whether you get a repair bill or a restoration bill.

Stop the water

Find the main shutoff where the water line enters the house, usually near the meter in the basement, and turn it fully off. Label it now so anyone in the house can find it later.

Cut the power near water

If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, leave the room and shut the breaker off only if you can reach it without standing in water.

Stop using drains

In a sewer backup, every flush and every load of laundry sends more water into the line. Hold off until a plumber has looked at it.

If you smell gas, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us for the plumbing side. No pipe repair is worth staying in a house that smells of gas.

Water in Westlake comes from Lake Erie

Cleveland Water draws its drinking water from Lake Erie, and Westlake's own city website sends water-bill payments to Cleveland Water. Its 2025 water quality report lists hardness, measured as calcium carbonate, between 105 and 130 with an average of 117, and a pH between 7.03 and 7.57. In plain terms, the water is moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, tankless units and faucet aerators.

That is the reason we recommend a yearly tank flush and an anode check on older heaters. It is also why water softener and filtration belongs on the list for homes that see scale on fixtures. Test your own tap water before spending money, because hardness at your fixture depends on your plumbing as well as the utility.

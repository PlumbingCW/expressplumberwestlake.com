Dover Center is the old center of Westlake. Before the city took its present name in 1940 it was Dover Village, and before that Dover Township, which was organized in 1810. A local history article places the Dover Center settlement at Dover Center Road and Center Ridge Road.

This page is for homes and small businesses around that crossing and along nearby Hilliard Boulevard. It explains what we can verify about the area, and how to approach plumbing in an established part of town.

What the sources say about Dover Center

The Encyclopedia of Cleveland History says Dover Village was incorporated in 1911, renamed Westlake in 1940 and became a city in 1957. A history of the city says the 1953 municipal building stood at the corner of Hilliard Boulevard and Dover Center Road. In 2009 the city adopted design guidelines for the Dover Village area to build on the mid-century modern style of the historic center.

Those facts tell us the area has a long history. They do not tell us when any particular house was built, or what is in its walls. We do not make that leap.

Plumbing in an established part of the city

The honest approach to an established area is to find out, not assume. When we are called to a house here, we ask when it was built, whether pipes have been changed, and what the symptoms are. If a drain keeps backing up, a camera inspection shows the pipe itself before anyone recommends a repair.

Sewer line repair covers what happens when a line is damaged, and repiping explains when replacing supply pipe makes sense. Neither is a guess about your house. Both start with looking.

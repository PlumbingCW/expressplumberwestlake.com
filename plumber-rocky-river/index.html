Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

Water damage grows quickly. These four steps take under a minute and often decide how big the repair becomes.

1. Shut off the main valve

Turn the main water shutoff, usually near the meter in the basement, clockwise until it stops.

2. Kill power to wet areas

Switch off breakers only if you can reach them from a dry spot. Leave the house if water is near the panel.

3. Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the leak and take photos for your records.

4. Call our 24/7 line

Dial (440) 468-0237 and tell dispatch what you see. We will guide the next step.

Housing in Rocky River and what it means for plumbing

The city's community profile describes housing that includes homes along the lake as well as newer cluster homes and townhouses. Rocky River became a village in 1903 and has grown through several eras of building since, so no single pipe material covers the whole city.

That mix changes how we work. A lakefront house may have older drain lines and a basement that is close to groundwater. A townhouse is more likely to have newer plastic drains but shared walls and stacks. We ask when the house was built and what you see before we say what we expect to find.

Basement water and the city sewer

Rocky River's sewer department page says its crews maintain and clean all city sewers, both storm and sanitary, and that crews are available 24 hours a day on an emergency basis. It also asks residents who find water in a basement to call the city's service garage before calling a plumber. The number it lists is 440-356-5630.

That advice is practical. If the public sewer is overloaded or blocked, a plumber cannot fix it from your side, and the city may need to act. If the city confirms the street sewer is clear, the problem is on your private line, and that is our work. We can then clear it through drain cleaning, check it with a camera and repair it through sewer line repair.

Sewage or water in the basement: do this first

Stop using water in the house, keep children and pets away from the affected area and move belongings off the floor. Call the city service garage as its page asks, then call (440) 468-0237 for the private side.

Dispatch answers 24/7, and a plumber can be scheduled the same day when the schedule allows.

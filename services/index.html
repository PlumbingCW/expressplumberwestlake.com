How to pick the right page

If water is moving and you cannot stop it, start with burst pipe repair or leak detection. If a drain is slow or backed up, start with drain cleaning, and move to sewer line cleaning when every fixture is affected.

If hot water is the issue, water heater repair covers the fault, while replacement covers a failed tank. Planned work such as a remodel, a new bathroom or a tankless upgrade belongs with plumbing installation and tankless water heaters.

What the emergency label means

Services marked as emergency can be dispatched around the clock. Planned jobs such as installations, repiping and water softeners are scheduled in the daytime, though dispatch still answers at night to take your call. Each service page explains what to do while you wait and how we handle the job.

Not sure which service you need?

Call and describe what you see, hear and smell. Our dispatchers triage the call, tell you what can wait and what cannot, and send the right plumber. You get a clear price before work starts.

What every service page includes

  • A plain explanation of the problem and what usually causes it.
  • What to do in the first minutes, before a plumber arrives.
  • The repair options and their trade-offs, including when not to repair.
  • Local notes where a city or utility source backs them up.
  • Questions people ask, answered directly.

Emergency pages also say what to shut off and when to leave the building. If you cannot tell which page fits, call and describe it. Dispatch will route you.

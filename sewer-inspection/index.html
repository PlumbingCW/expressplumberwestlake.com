Sewer Inspection in Westlake, OH

A camera inspection shows what is inside a sewer line, so you decide on repairs from evidence instead of guesses. Call (440) 468-0237 to schedule, answered 24/7.

What a sewer camera shows

We feed a waterproof camera on a flexible rod through a cleanout or an opened fixture. The camera shows the pipe's interior in real time, including its material, joints, roots, cracks, bellies and buildup. A locator on the camera head pinpoints depth and position so any repair can be targeted.

You see what we see. That is the point of the inspection. Instead of being told a line needs to be replaced, you watch the picture and hear the options.

When an inspection makes sense

Buying a house

Older sewer laterals are a common hidden cost. A camera look before closing gives you something concrete to negotiate with.

Repeat backups

If cable work keeps clearing the line only for a short time, the camera finds the underlying cause.

Before a repair

Lining, spot repair and replacement all start with knowing where the problem is and how bad it is.

What you receive after the visit

  • A plain description of what the camera found and where, measured from the cleanout.
  • Whether the pipe is a candidate for cleaning, lining, spot repair or replacement.
  • A clear price for any repair before work starts, with no obligation to go ahead.

Why Westlake homes are a good match for camera work

Westlake includes older neighborhoods around the historic center, newer developments and commercial areas such as Crocker Park and the Columbia Road corridor. The sewer materials and ages differ across these, so a one-size repair recommendation rarely fits. Seeing the pipe lets us match the repair to the line.

The camera also helps after heavy rain. If a basement has taken on water, we can check whether the sewer lateral is part of the cause or the water came from elsewhere. See basement waterproofing and sump pump service for the other common sources.

If the camera finds a clog or buildup rather than damage, we can often clear the line during the same visit. If it finds damage, sewer line repair covers the options.

How to prepare for the visit

  • Know where your cleanout is. It is usually a capped pipe near the foundation or in the basement.
  • Clear a path to it and move stored items so the technician has room to work.
  • Tell us what you have noticed and when, including smells and which fixtures act up.
  • If you are buying the house, send the address and ask for a copy of the findings.

Reading the results

What the camera showsWhat it meansUsual next step
Clean pipe, normal jointsThe line is in good shapeNo work needed
Grease, scale or paper buildupThe pipe is restricted but intactCable or jet cleaning
Roots at jointsJoints are open and roots will returnCut roots, then consider repair
Cracks, offset or collapsed sectionThe pipe is damagedSpot repair, lining or replacement

A line with a belly will show standing water in the picture. That is not always urgent, but it explains repeat clogs and is useful to know before you buy a house.

Many inspections end with good news. A line that looks sound today gives you a record to compare against later, and it tells you that a recurring clog is a habit problem rather than a pipe problem.

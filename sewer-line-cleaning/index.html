How to tell it is the main line

One slow sink is a branch problem. A toilet that gurgles when the washer drains, a shower that bubbles when you flush and a basement floor drain that rises with the rest are signs the whole system is stuck at the main.

Main line cleaning reaches the pipe between the house and the connection to the city sewer. We start at the cleanout if the house has one, usually near the foundation or in the basement.

The cleaning process

  1. Locate the cleanout and protect the floor around it.
  2. Run a cable machine with a cutting head to open the line.
  3. Flush the pipe with water and watch the flow.
  4. Use a camera or hydro jetting if the blockage keeps returning.

For lines with heavy grease or roots, hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall more completely than a cable. If the cable keeps hitting the same point, a camera tells us whether the pipe is damaged there. Our sewer inspection shows it.

What sewer backups look like in older neighborhoods

Older lines in Westlake can be clay, cast iron or an early plastic. They collect roots at the joints and scale on the walls, and they sag where the soil settles. A backup in a finished basement is the most costly way to learn this.

If you have already had water in the basement, keep the affected area clear and do not run the washer until the line is checked. Rocky River, for example, asks residents with water in a basement to call its sewer service garage before a plumber, because the city handles the public sewer. Check with your own city for the same kind of guidance.

Main line backup tonight: what to do

Stop all water use. Do not flush toilets and do not run the dishwasher or washer. Move belongings off the basement floor and keep children and pets away from the drain area.

Keeping the main line clear after cleaning

Grease, wipes and heavy paper are the usual culprits for repeat blockages. Keep them out of the drain and the interval between cleanings grows. If roots are the cause, a scheduled cleaning or a repair is the real fix. Tell us what you found and we will recommend the lightest option.

Cable or jet for a main line

A cable machine is the usual first tool because it opens the line quickly. If the cable pulls back with grease or scale on it, or the line clogs again in a short time, jetting is the better follow-up because it cleans what the cable leaves on the pipe wall. We explain which one we used and why, so you know what to expect next time.

What to watch for after cleaning

Run the fixtures that were affected and watch for gurgling or slow drainage over the next few days. A line that is clear for a short time and then slows again has a cause the cleaning did not reach. Call back and ask about a camera inspection.
